02 — ABOUT

About Me

Hello! I'm Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan, , an aspiring AI researcher and academic with a strong passion for developing intelligent systems that solve real-world problems. I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering , Faculty of Engineering, University of Moratuwa . I completed my Bachelor’s Honours in Mechatronics at the Department of Science and Technology , Faculty of Applied Sciences, Uva Wellassa University of Sri Lanka . I previously worked as an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Information and Communication Technology , Faculty of Technological Studies, University of Vavuniya , Sri Lanka and worked as a Graduate Teaching Assistant at the Department of Mechanical and Manufacturing Technology , Faculty of Technology, Wayamba University of Sri Lanka . I completed my industrial training as a Trainee Mechatronics Engineer at Milco (Private) Limited , Digana. My research interests include Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Mechatronics, with a focus on solving real-world problems. My goal is to continue developing as a researcher and contribute to innovative, reliable, and intelligent solutions that create meaningful real-world impact.

Projects

3-D Design of Ice Cream Cup and Cone Filler Machine

Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan | May 2023 – Sep 2023

This project involved creating 3D and 2D drawings of an ice cream cup and cone filler machine using SolidWorks. The design included the cup's cylindrical shape, the cone filler, handles, and textures. Additionally, the project required measuring the machine to ensure accurate sizing for reinstallation.

Skills: SolidWorks, 3D Design

Cow Feeder Supporter

Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan | May 2023 – Sep 2023

This project involved the development and manufacturing of a cattle feed stand for efficient cow feeding. Metal bars were used to construct a durable structure through cutting, welding, grinding, and painting processes. The completed feeding system was designed to securely hold and dispense feed while reducing wastage and maintaining an organized and hygienic feeding environment.

Skills: Mechanical Fabrication, Welding, Manufacturing

Jet Ink Text Printed & Non-Printed Yogurt Lids Detection System

Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan | May 2023 – Sep 2023

This project involved developing a system to detect inkjet-printed and non-printed yogurt lids. An RGB sensor and Arduino were used to detect printed information on yogurt lids, including the expiry date, manufacturing date, price, and batch number.

Skills: Arduino, C++, RGB Sensor

PCB Design of Fodder Maker Control Circuit

Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan | May 2023 – Sep 2023

This project focused on designing a control circuit for a fodder-making system. The original circuit was implemented using a dot board and was redesigned as a PCB using KiCad. The control system was designed to manage lighting, water, humidity, and CO₂ levels.

Skills: PCB Design, KiCad

Sugar Particle Separator

Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan | May 2023 – Sep 2023

A sugar particle separation system was developed to separate sugar according to particle size. The mechanical structure was designed using SolidWorks, while vibrating sensors positioned at the edges of the separator disk were used to detect and classify particles.

Skills: SolidWorks, Arduino

Deep Learning-Based Traffic Sign Recognition and Auditory Alert System for Sri Lankan Drivers

Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan | Oct 2022 – May 2023

A deep learning-based traffic sign recognition system was developed for Sri Lankan drivers. Traffic sign images were labelled and preprocessed using Roboflow. YOLOv5 was used to recognize 15 traffic sign classes, while PyDub was used to generate real-time voice alerts.

F1 Score: 90.09%   |   mAP: 87.55%

Skills: Python, YOLOv5, CNN, Roboflow, PyDub, Deep Learning
