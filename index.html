About me

Hello! I'm Geerthana Vimalambikaipakan, an aspiring AI researcher and academic with an interdisciplinary background spanning Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Mechatronics, IoT, Robotics, and Intelligent Systems. I enjoy building data-driven solutions with machine learning, deep learning, automation, and embedded technologies. My work includes computer vision projects such as real-time object detection, as well as Arduino-based robotics and IoT systems. I bring strong analytical, problem-solving, and research skills, and I work well across disciplines to apply emerging technologies to practical challenges.

I am currently pursuing a Master’s degrees in Data Science & AI at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, University of Moratuwa. In this programme, I am building hands-on experience in machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision, applying them to practical problems. I completed my Bachelor's Honours degree in Mechatronics at the Department of Science and Technology, Faculty of Applied Sciences, Uva Wellassa University of Sri Lanka. Through this degree, I gained an interdisciplinary foundation in mechanical, electronic, and computing systems, including embedded systems, robotics, and automation.

I previously worked as an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Information and Communication Technology, Faculty of Technological Studies, University of Vavuniya, Sri Lanka. In this role, I delivered tutorials and supervised laboratory sessions across multiple modules in computing and information technology. I developed lab guides and instructional resources, and contributed to continuous and final assessments, including assignments, quizzes, lab reports, and examinations. Before that I worked as a Temporary Demonstrator at the Department of Mechanical and Manufacturing Technology, Faculty of Technology, Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. During my time there, I facilitated and supervised laboratory and tutorial sessions across multiple engineering modules. I prepared lab manuals and instructional materials, and graded assignments, quizzes, lab reports, and examinations.

Earlier, I completed my industrial training as a Trainee Mechatronics Engineer at Milco (Private) Limited, Digana, Kandy, Sri Lanka. Throughout the training, I gained multidisciplinary exposure across manufacturing, industrial, electrical, electronics, mechanical, and mechatronics engineering. My work covered boiler operations, chiller plant systems, electrical distribution, PCB design, CAD/CAM (2D and 3D), embedded systems development, and refrigeration mechanics.

My research interests include Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Mechatronics, with a focus on applied problems. I aim to keep growing as a researcher and contribute reliable, intelligent solutions that create meaningful impact.
